Questions

Frequently asked

Which security audits do you hold?

We are SOC 2 Type II and SOC 1 Type II audited, we pass IRS audits, and our staff and facilities are HIPAA trained and compliant. Type II matters: it tests whether controls actually operated over a period of time, rather than whether they existed on one day.

Can you handle both printed mail and digital delivery?

Yes. The same data file drives your printed pieces, secure email PDF delivery, and an online bill pay portal — so you are not integrating with one vendor for paper and another for digital.

How do you make sure each document reaches the right person?

Camera and vision systems read each document at every production stage and match it against its envelope before sealing. NCOA and CASS processing verifies the address itself, so both the contents and the destination are confirmed before a piece leaves the floor.

How does outsourcing save us money?

Three ways: USPS workshare presort discounts we pass back to you, the equipment and labor you no longer staff internally, and the reprint and return postage that NCOA and CASS address processing prevents. We will model all three against your actual volume during the strategy session.

Do you serve clients outside Kansas City?

Yes. Print-to-mail outsourcing runs nationwide across the US. Mail pickup for presorting and outsourced mailing services is available within the greater Kansas City area.

How are our documents backed up?

Our disaster recovery service keeps digital backups of your critical documents on secure servers, so your records stay independently recoverable and available whenever you need them.
