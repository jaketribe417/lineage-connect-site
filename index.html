The Problems We Solve

Lineage Connect addresses four common business mailing failures:

  • Insecure Data Infrastructure — No risk mitigation for storage, tracking, and location of physical and digital data.
  • No Trusted or Experienced Partners — Lack of a mailing partner versed in compliance and regulations for sensitive-information industries.
  • Equipment Skill & Knowledge Gaps — Inadequate labor and expertise to operate equipment and handle core business process volumes.
  • Unclear Business Plan — No clear affordability, capability, or vision for outsourcing versus in-house mailing systems.

Why Choose Lineage Connect?

  • 30+ Years of Experience — Facility infrastructure built to meet HIPAA and essential security/compliance requirements.
  • National Coverage — Full outsourcing functionality across the US.
  • End-to-End Equipped — Systems, equipment, and storage capabilities handle mail from creation to distribution.
  • USPS Workshare Value — Exclusive mailing discounts passed back to clients via a workshare partnership with the post office.

Security, Compliance & Disaster Recovery

"Bad things happen when things go wrong with your business's mailing system."

Risks of insecure mail: Customers' private information ending up in wrong hands, late delivery, fines, damaged brand reputation, and lost clients.

Lineage Connect safeguards information by:

  • Keeping data on secure servers
  • Securely tracking mail from print to delivery
  • Offering disaster recovery services — digital backups stored securely so documents survive worst-case scenarios

Core Services

Outsourced Print-To-Mail Services

Full-service handling of organization, processing, printing, finishing, envelope insertion, postage, and delivery to USPS. Guarantees mail arrives to the right person, at the right time, the correct way.

Outsourced Mailing Services

High-volume jobs including sorting, presorting, metering, and USPS delivery. May include mail pickup within greater Kansas City limits.

Local Presorting Services

Address processing, metering, and presorting through sorters to earn USPS discounts via the workshare partnership. May include mail pickup within greater Kansas City limits.

Disaster Recovery Services

Secure digital document backups stored on secure servers for business continuity.
